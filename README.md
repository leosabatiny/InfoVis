# Powering Europe's Future: An Analysis of the Sustainable Energy Transition

This project explores Europe's complex journey towards sustainable energy. It aims to understand the delicate balance between reaching ambitious climate goals and the practical economic and social realities of this major change.

We look at the energy transition from two key perspectives:

* **Economic and Social Feasibility:** This view examines whether Europe can truly afford and manage this energy shift. We consider the costs involved, the financial ability of different countries, and potential challenges like access to funding, the availability of raw materials, and the need for new infrastructure.
* **Climate Urgency:** This perspective highlights why this transition is so critically needed right now. It focuses on the severe impacts of climate change, such as rising temperatures affecting agriculture, more extreme weather events, and changes to natural ecosystems, along with the urgent deadlines set by scientists for reducing emissions.

To explore these themes, we use several publicly available datasets, including:
* Monthly electricity production statistics, showing the mix of energy sources.
* Comprehensive data on CO2 and other greenhouse gas emissions, along with economic indicators like GDP.
* Monthly wholesale electricity price data across European countries, giving insight into market costs.

Through data preparation, statistical analysis, and clear visualizations, we aim to uncover key patterns, challenges, and opportunities in Europe's move to renewable energy. Our findings are supported by insights from scientific research.

This repository holds all the code, steps for data processing, and visual materials used to create an interactive Jupyter Book website that presents our full research.
